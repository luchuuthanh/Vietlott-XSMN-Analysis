## Caveats and next work

- These are secondary result publishers, not the lottery issuers' signed/official result records.
- This pass verifies 162 prize rows (9 of 402 station-days; 2.24% of station-days) in the 2026-07-01 to 2026-11-05 supplement.
- It does **not** certify the remaining 393 station-days / 7,074 rows.
- The structural normalization pipeline passed separately (103,356 rows, 21 stations, 1,827 calendar days, no duplicate keys, no schedule gaps). Structural validity is not the same as result correctness.
- Keep the supplement marked third-party / partially verified until the remaining station-days are checked. Do not describe the entire five-year dataset as officially verified.
