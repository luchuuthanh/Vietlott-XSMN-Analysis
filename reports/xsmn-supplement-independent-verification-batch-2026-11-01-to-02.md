## Caveats
These are secondary result-table sources, not certified primary records from each provincial lottery company. This batch establishes cross-source agreement for the checked station-days; it does not establish complete independent verification of the entire 383.im supplement. Keep source provenance and unverified station-days visible. Do not infer that unexamined rows are verified.
