# XSMN supplement independent verification — batch 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-22

## Scope
This is one 10-calendar-day batch, inclusive: 2026-10-13 through 2026-10-22. It covers 31 station-days and 558 prize-result rows in the normalized 383.im supplement. Each station's nine prize classes were compared as sets, ignoring display order within a prize class and preserving leading zeroes.

## Primary full-table comparison
The full prize tables for all 10 dates were compared against the October 2026 historical result archive at:
- https://www.kqxsmt.net/xsmn/10-2026.html
- https://xosomiennam.online/xsmn-30-ngay.html

The second archive page provides full tables for 2026-10-13 through 2026-10-22 in its historical results section. The normalized supplement matched the tables for every station and prize class in this batch.

## Source anomalies found and disposition
These anomalies were in secondary websites, not in the normalized dataset:
1. A ketqua.vn result page for 2026-10-21 displayed Sóc Trăng jackpot 281683, while the full tables on KQXS.vn and the October archive show 281682. The normalized supplement has 281682 and is retained.
2. An xsmm.vn page for 2026-10-18 displayed Kiên Giang G3 number 33429, while KQXS.vn and the October archive show 33439. The normalized supplement has 33439 and is retained.
3. xosohn.com result for 2026-10-17 renders split digits that conflict with the complete result tables on SXMB and XSKT.net; exclude this site from validation.

These are logged as source-quality warnings; do not alter canonical rows based on an isolated inconsistent secondary page.

## Result
- 10/10 calendar dates covered.
- 31/31 station-days checked.
- 558/558 prize-result rows matched the full historical archive tables.
- No discrepancy between the normalized supplement and the selected full-table references was found.
- Leading zeroes were preserved (examples include 0148, 00261, 048, 00319 and 041493).

## Cumulative coverage
Including previously checked dates 2026-10-23 through 2026-11-05:
- 75/402 station-days (18.66%)

## Caveats and remaining work
The sources are secondary result-table websites, not certified primary records issued directly by each provincial lottery company. A full-table match to these sources is not the same as primary-source certification. 327 station-days / 5,886 result rows remain to be checked. Keep the 383.im provenance and verification status explicit.
